5 security tips for September

Hi reader,

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adoption among our readers hit 64% in 2025, up from 41% in 2023. Here is how to protect the rest of your accounts:

  1. Turn on 2FA everywhere it is offered.
  2. Use a password manager and a unique password for every site.
  3. Confirm the sender before you click any link in a sign-in email.
  4. Replace SMS with an authenticator app when you can.
  5. Review your connected apps at least every 90 days.
